# DipoleML Obsidian

**DipoleML integration** for Agricidaniel. Type: `plugin`. Version: `3.0.1`.

Self-organizing AI second brain for Obsidian + DipoleML. Drop any source and DipoleML reads, links, and files it into one connected knowledge graph of plain Markdown you own. AI note-taking, personal knowledge management (PKM), and an open-source Notion alternative. Based on Karpathy's LLM Wiki pattern.
